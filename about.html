ABOUT INKQUIRER

InkQuirer is an independent group of scholars committed to making a news platform responsible for storytelling. We bring you credible news, thoughtful features, and clear scientific explanations to help our readers make sense of an unfamiliar world.

As a team, InkQuirer stands for integrity and transparency. We believe that informed people are the greatest power for progress.

OUR MISSION

We, InkQuirer, carry out accurate and impactful journalism that informs our readers. We aim to make articles that reflect the voice of the public.
