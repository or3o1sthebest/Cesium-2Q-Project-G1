Zaldy Co questions PBBM over flood control budget

Former House of Representatives member Zaldy Salcedo Co clarified that all changes to the agency funding were approved by President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr, assisted by Secretary Mina Pangandaman.

In response to the President’s confusion over the flood control budget, he released a video on November 15 explaining the reason behind all budget adjustments.

Zaldy Co said, “Sinabi pa ni Sec. Mina, Everything is cleared with the President. Basta importante, pasok ang pondo na request ng Pangulo. The following day, sinabi ni Sec. Mina, approved na si Presidente BBM sa lahat na binawasan na ahensya. Masaya na ang Pangulo dahil naipasok ang gusto niyang 100 billion insertion.”

Additionally, he emphasized that approximately 25% of the 100 billion insertion was allocated for President Marcos himself.

“Ang ibig sabihin ito, 25% ng 100 billion ang SOP na kailangan ibigay kay BBM mismo. In total, 25 billion ang napunta kay Pangulong Bongbong Marcos,” Co noted.

Zaldy Co also quoted President Marcos’ words on his SONA to ask for President Marcos’ accountability and transparency.

Co explained, “Sabi nga ni BBM nung SONA, let’s do it right at mahiya naman kayo, hindi po ba?”
