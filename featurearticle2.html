Hello travelers of a foreign land I’ll be your guide today now before we head to the main attraction. First we will have to go down memory lane to truly appreciate its wonderfulness and beauty.Koronadal city a place of welcome and comfort this city may be small trust me on this they have a brighter future ahead.

The first thing on our tour is the Roundball. This is the city’s centerpiece literally and figuratively. The roundball serves as a roundabout guiding cars on their paths and guiding us people. The see up there is none other than our beloved General Paulino Santos. General Paulino Santos was a well respected man who fought against the Moro Piracy here in MIndanao. He led the first 200 migrants from Luzon and Visayas here in Koronadal City. He was commemorated in General Santos city which was named after him but here in Kornadal we decided to give our own remembrance with him being the statue in our city’s centerpiece.

Now hop-on to Koronadal’s primary transportation, the tricycles!! Welcome to our City’s city hall, the seat of our wondrous Mayor. The architecture is nothing truly impressive but this marks how Koronadal has come so far from a tiny city that was just newly built to now a city built on the dreams and hopes of its citizens.

A traveler raises his hand “Uhmm Sir? May I ask what that sound in the distance sounds like a parade”.

Oh, that? no worries, that's our next destination. Ok everybody go on your tricycles and let's get moving.

We have arrived at what all of you have been waiting for, welcome to the Hinugyaw festival. Let me introduce to you our breathtaking festivities. The Parade-Mardi gras competition, a parade to honor the agriculture cultivated in our lovely city and truly brings its essence and importance to life.

While the Hinugyaw festival is going on we also celebrate the 9th Indigenous Peoples Day on January 9 to commemorate the achievements and contributions of Indigenous people. We celebrate IP (Indigenous people) day as they are also part of the Koronadal Family so it only makes sense they are recognized.

You can also expect some goodies at our Mayor Nights Raffle though you have to live here in Koronadal, but it's not too late to live here and appreciate this city's atmosphere.

The Hinugyaw festival not only serves as a festival to commemorate our city’s creation but also reflects the Koronadaleños unwavering passion and strength. To stand for 85 years is a feat of strength, Oh Koronodal stand proud you are strong. May this city prosper to its light and darkest moments. Happy and enjoy the Hinugyaw Festival
