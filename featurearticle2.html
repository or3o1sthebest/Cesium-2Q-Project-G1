Recorded by DOST-PHIVOLCS Philippine Seismic Network (PSN), a rare case of a doublet earthquake rocked the southeastern coast of Manay, Davao Oriental, affecting several provinces within the vicinity, on October 10.

The first quake with a magnitude of 7.4 was quickly followed by a separate 6.8 magnitude shock, raising tsunami warnings for the nearby seven provinces and areas.

President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. said, “to our kababayans in the affected regions, please stay alert and calm. Move to higher ground and stay away from the shore until authorities declare it safe. Follow all instructions from your local disaster councils and barangay officials. Your safety is our top priority.”

Additionally, the pair of tremors was felt in regions of Mindanao measured at the intensity of V and with a depth of 20 kilometers.

“The quake tectonic in the region had a recorded depth of 20 kilometers. Intensity V was felt here in Davao City, while many other areas in eastern Mindanao also experienced the powerful tremor,” Correspondent Hernel Tocmo of ABS-CBN News stated.

Occurring at the same area within a short period, the event was classified as a doublet earthquake by the Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology (PHIVOLCS).

PHIVOLCS explained, “they are distinct or different earthquakes in almost the same area with two (or more) main shocks that have slight difference in magnitude.”

Moreover, as reported by the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC)on October 14, the doublet earthquake affected over a million of individuals.
